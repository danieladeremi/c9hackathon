Projected lane gold vs the real team gold lead at 14 minutes (2026, 448 games): correlation 0.159, slope 1.048 real gold per projected gold.

A typical draft (one standard deviation of projected lane gold) moves a 50% game by about -0.7 points in M1.

**Power check.** A real gold lead at 14 minutes is worth 0.666 logit per 1,000 gold (2025). If a projected lead converted the same way, a typical draft would move the bar 5.3 points and improve log loss by about 0.0057 per game. The observed interval rules that out.

**Verdict:** The draft does not add a measurable held-out improvement over team strength: no moving bar. Show the pre-game number and the projected lane table instead. The test had the power to see the effect a projected gold lead would have if it converted like a real one, and rules it out: draft lane advantages show up at 14 minutes but do not turn into wins.
