## Pick WPA, and how much of it is signal

3,474 team-games scored with out-of-fold win probabilities (5-fold, grouped by game), 322 champion-role pairs.

**Reliability check.** For each per-champion metric: the correlation of champion-role averages between two halves of the games. *Random halves* (5 random splits, at least 30 games per half) measures sampling noise alone; *by season* (2025 vs 2026, at least 20 games per half) adds real meta change. Near 0 means the metric is noise at this sample size.

| Raw win rate | +0.23 | 70 | +0.18 | 53 |
| Lane gold diff @14 | +0.73 | 69 | +0.71 | 53 |

**Shrinkage.** Each figure is shrunk toward its role average by empirical Bayes, keeping a share tau^2 / (tau^2 + se^2) of its observed difference (its *reliability*). The evidence label shown to coaches is *strong* at reliability >= 0.6, *some* at >= 0.3, otherwise *weak*. A metric whose random split-half r is below 0.3 is shown as weak everywhere: WPA 15-25 min, WPA 25+ min, WPA total, Raw win rate.
